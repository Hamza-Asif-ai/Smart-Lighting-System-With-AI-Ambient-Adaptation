Method:
  - Chronological simulation: 30 days x 288 steps/day (8,640 steps, 5 min each).
  - Energy per step = Watts x step_minutes / 60 (Wh); summed and /1000 -> kWh.
  - One 60 W-equivalent LED bulb; power assumed linear in brightness (20%=12 W, 50%=30 W, 80%=48 W, 90%=54 W).
  - Tariff ASSUMPTION: PKR 25 per kWh (replace with your bill).
